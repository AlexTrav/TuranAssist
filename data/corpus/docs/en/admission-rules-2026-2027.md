1 GENERAL REGULATIONS
1.1 This internal regulatory act (hereinafter referred to as the Rules) is part of the structure of
organizational documents of the institution “Turan University” (hereinafter referred to as the
University) and was developed in accordance with subparagraph 11 of Article 5 of the Law
of the Republic of Kazakhstan dated July 27, 2007 "On Education", subparagraph 1 of Article
10 of the Law of the Republic of Kazakhstan "On Public Services" dated April 15, 2013 and
on the basis of the following regulatory legal acts of the Republic of Kazakhstan on issues of
admission to study at universities:
- Standard rules for admission to study in educational organizations implementing educational
programs of higher and postgraduate education, approved by the order of the Ministry of
Higher Education of the Republic of Kazakhstan dated October 31, 2018 No. 600 (as amended
by the order of the Ministry of Higher Education of the Republic of Kazakhstan dated July 8,
2025 No. 341, with amendments);
- Rules for awarding an educational grant to pay for higher and postgraduate education with
the award of a bachelor's or master's degree, approved by the order of the Ministry of Higher
Education of the Republic of Kazakhstan dated June 19, 2025, No. 315;
- Rules for conducting a unified national testing and providing public services “Issuance of a
certificate of passing a unified national testing”, approved by the order of the Ministry of
Higher Education of the Republic of Kazakhstan dated May 2, 2017 No. 204;
- Rules for conducting comprehensive testing, approved by order of the Ministry of Science
and Higher Education of the Republic of Kazakhstan dated May 8, 2019, No. 190, with the
latest amendments introduced by the order of the Acting Minister of Science and Higher
Education of the Republic of Kazakhstan dated November 1, 2024No. 505;
- The list of groups of educational programs that determine the socio-economic development
of the village, for which a quota for admission to higher and postgraduate education
organizations of the Republic of Kazakhstan is provided, approved by order of the Ministry
of Higher Education of the Republic of Kazakhstan dated May 30, 2019 No. 247;
- Methodological recommendations for the conformity of related groups of educational
programs of higher education and specialties of technical and vocational, post-secondary
education" dated April 1, 2019, approved by the order of the Ministry of Higher Education of
the Republic of Kazakhstan dated April 1, 2019 No. 134;
- The size of the admission quota for admission to study in educational organizations
implementing higher education programs, approved by the order of the Ministry of Higher
Education of the Republic of Kazakhstan dated July 26, 2023 No. 357;
- State educational order for the training of personnel with higher or postgraduate education,
in educational organizations financed from the republican budget (with the exception of
educational organizations that train specialists for the Armed Forces of the Republic of
Kazakhstan, other troops and military formations, as well as special state bodies), for the 20242025, 2025-2026, 2026-2027 academic years, approved by the order of the Ministry of
Education and Science of the Republic of Kazakhstan;
- State educational order for the training of personnel with higher and (or) postgraduate
education in the context of groups of educational programs for the 2024-2025, 2025-2026,
2026-2027 academic years, approved by the order of the Ministry of Higher Education of the
Republic of Kazakhstan.
1.2 These Rules define the procedure for admission to Turan University for study in educational
programs of bachelor's, master's, doctoral PhD, MBA, EMBA, DBA.

1.3 Admission of persons entering the Turan University for study in higher education programs
is carried out through the placement of an educational grant for higher education at the expense
of the republican budget or the local budget, as well as payment for tuition at the expense of
the student’s own funds and other sources.
1.4 Admission of persons entering the Turan University for study in postgraduate educational
programs (Master's degree, PhD) is carried out through the placement of a state educational
order for the training of personnel in scientific and pedagogical fields, as well as payment for
tuition from the students' own funds and other sources.
1.5 Admission of foreign citizens to bachelor's, master's and doctoral degree programs is carried
out on a fee-paying basis.
The receipt by foreigners on a competitive basis in accordance with the state educational order
of free education in educational programs of bachelor's, master's and doctoral degrees is
determined by international treaties of the Republic of Kazakhstan, with the exception of the
scholarship program for master's programs.
Foreign citizens are accepted to MBA, EMBA, and DBA programs in accordance with the
procedure established by the legislation of the Republic of Kazakhstan and international
treaties.

2.2 Procedure for admission and conducting creative examinations
2.2.1. To organize and conduct creative examinations, by decision of the Rector of Turan University,
an examination committee is created for the duration of the examination.
The commission includes representatives from the university's faculty, public organizations,
and the media. The creative examination commission for the Arts program includes
individuals with relevant education in the relevant field and recommended by the local
authorized body in the field of culture and sports.
The committee consists of an odd number of examinees, and a committee chairperson is
elected by a majority vote of its members. A committee decision is considered valid if at least
two-thirds of its members are present at the meeting. The committee's decision is made by a
majority vote of those present at the examination. In the event of a tie, the committee
chairperson's vote is decisive.

with video and/or audio recording.

2.2.10. Creative exams for applicants with documents on general secondary or technical and
vocational, post-secondary education are assessed on a 50-point scale.
The creative exam for applicants to related higher education programs that provide for
shortened study periods is assessed on a 20-point scale.
2.2.11. The results of the creative examination are documented in a score sheet and a committee report
in any format, and are submitted to the Admissions Committee's responsible secretary for
announcing the results. The committee report is signed by the chairperson and all present
committee members.

2.3 Applications for participation in the competition for an educational grant
2.3.1. Educational grants are awarded on a competitive basis for specific state educational programs
in accordance with the scores of the UNT certificates in the order of the groups of educational
programs declared by applicants, with the issuance of a certificate of award of an educational
grant.
If the core subjects coincide, the applicant can indicate up to four GOP, with the exception of
applicants to GOP that require creative training.
2.3.2. To participate in the competition for a higher education grant funded by the republican or
local budget, a UNT certificate is required. Applicants must submit one of two UNT results
and have the right to choose the certificate with the highest test result (UNT certificates with
different unique numbers).

4.2.14 During the period of entrance examinations for doctoral studies, the university creates

It is permitted to create one examination committee for related educational programs within

4.2.15 Examination committees for groups of educational programs are formed from among the

4.2.16 Applicants complete an application form indicating their last name, first name, and
patronymic (if applicable), university, and state educational program. They then proceed to

questions related to the academic program group, and submit their results to the university of
their choice. The results of the readiness test are processed by the National Testing Center.
4.2.17 The university's examination committees check the applicant's work using their individual
code and send the results through the information system to the NTC for subsequent
publication of the results.
4.2.18 The Ministry of Higher Education of the Republic of Kazakhstan announces the results the

The certificate of the entrance examination held in the period from August 4 to 20 is valid
until December 1 of the current calendar year;
The certificate for the entrance examination held between November 18 and December 11 is
valid until March 1 of the following calendar year.

The interview is aimed at assessing the applicant's professional and personal qualities, and
their potential for conducting research or experimental research work.

4.3 Work of appeal commissions
4.3.1 To review appeals from individuals who disagree with the results of their entrance
examinations, the university establishes appeals committees. The chairpersons and members
of the appeals committees are approved by order of the chairperson of the Admissions
Committee.
4.3.2 The Appeals Committee accepts and considers applications from applicants to doctoral studies
based on the content of examination materials and technical reasons.The Appeals Commission
and/or the Republican Appeals Commission make a decision on the application of the person
appealing the results of the entrance examination.
4.3.3 Applicants applying for a doctoral degree must submit an appeal to the chairperson of the
appeals committee in person or through the NCT information system. Applicants must submit
appeals until 1:00 PM the day following the announcement of their doctoral entrance exam
results and must be reviewed by the university's appeals committee within one day of
submitting their application.When the appeal committee considers the application, the person
filing the appeal provides an identity document.
4.3.4 Applications for appeal of the computerized entrance exam for doctoral studies are accepted
the following day from 1:00 PM to 1:40 PM after the results are announced, at the location of
the organization determined by the authorized body in the field of education.
4.3.4 You are allotted 40 minutes to appeal the content of the entrance exam for doctoral studies,
including: 1) 10 minutes for the test section to determine readiness for doctoral studies; 2) 30
minutes for the essay writing section and answers to exam questions on the profile of the
educational program group.
Appeals regarding the content of test assignments and technical reasons from applicants to
doctoral studies in the TGO block and additional testing of English language proficiency are
considered by the Republican Appeals Commission.
An application for technical reasons for an appeal from persons applying for doctoral studies
in the TGO block and additional testing of English proficiency is submitted by the applicant
during the testing.

4.4 Admission to doctoral programs
4.4.1 Admission to doctoral studies is based on an international certificate confirming proficiency
in a foreign language in accordance with the Common European Framework of Reference for
Languages and the results of an entrance examination in the profile of the doctoral educational
program group.
4.4.2 Persons who score at least 75 points on the entrance exam are admitted to doctoral studies on
a fee-paying basis and under a state educational order on a competitive basis.
4.4.3 In the event of a tie in competitive scores, preference for admission to doctoral studies goes
to those with the highest score in the academic program's core group. Academic achievements
relevant to the academic program's core group are then taken into account: academic
publications, including those in top-ranked scientific journals ranked in the 1st and 2nd
quartiles according to the Journal Citation Reports of Clarivate Analytics' Web of Science
database over the past three calendar years; certificates of scientific development; certificates
of awarding academic scholarships and grants; and certificates/diplomas for participation in
academic conferences and competitions.
4.4.4 Unfilled places under the state educational order for doctoral studies, including targeted ones,
are returned to the authorized bodies in the field of education, healthcare and culture in the
form of an application for further redistribution between higher education institutions within
the groups of postgraduate educational programs by September 5 of the calendar year.
4.4.5 The University submits to the Ministry of Higher Education of the Republic of Kazakhstan
within 10 calendar days a final report on the organization and conduct of admission, as well
as copies of orders for admission to doctoral studies under the state educational order.
