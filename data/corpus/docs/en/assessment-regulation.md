1 GENERAL PROVISIONS
1. This internal local regulatory act (hereinafter referred to as the Regulation) included in
the structure of organizational documents of Turan University institution, was developed in
accordance with the Law of the Republic of Kazakhstan “About Education”, dated July 27,
2007 (with amendments and additions), and secondary, other regulatory legal acts in the field of
education of the Republic of Kazakhstan and it determines the procedure for conducting current
control of academic performance, intermediate and final certification of students.
2. The current control of academic performance, intermediate and final certification are conducted
in order to determine the degree of mastering educational programs and state mandatory
standards of higher and postgraduate education approved by the order no. 24 of the Minister of
Science and Higher Education of the Republic of Kazakhstan, dated July 20, 2022 (hereinafter –
SES).
3. During the introduction of restrictive measures (to prevent the spread of coronavirus
infection, etc.), Turan University (hereinafter referred to as the University) ensures the
implementation of OP in a remote format in accordance with the relevant regulatory legal acts
of the Republic of Kazakhstan.
4. The University provides students and teaching staff (hereinafter referred to as TS) with
access to distance educational technologies, systems for conducting online classes, for
completing and submitting assignments in electronic form, checking them for plagiarism.
5. The teaching staff ensures that the LMS Canvas is filled with academic and
methodological materials of disciplines (syllabuses, tasks for students, literature, video lectures,
and reference information), timely conducts online classes and checks assignments.
6. The University develops and provides students and teaching staff with access to the
instructions for using distance educational technologies (hereinafter-DET) and to the academic
policy, including the evaluation policy of intermediate and final certification on the university's
website.
7. The University provides timely technical support for the educational process, including
the possibility of using asynchronous and flexible approaches for students that are in situations
that limit their synchronous participation to online learning
8. The University ensures the confidentiality of students’ and teaching staff’ data when
using applications and platforms
9. The procedure for conducting the current control of academic performance, intermediate
and final certification of students is determined in accordance with the academic and the
evaluation policy of the university.
10. Evaluation of learning outcomes is based on the principles of academic integrity. The
rules of academic integrity are presented in the “Academic Code of “Turan” university student’s
behavior” section of Student Code.
The university expels the student if he/she violates the principles of the academic integrity
rules.
The following definitions are used in this Regulation:
1) Academic integrity is a set of values and principles that express the student's honesty in
studying, when performing written works (tests, essays, theses, and dissertations), answering
exams, researching, expressing his/her position, in relationships with academic staff, teachers
and other students, as well as in evaluation;
2) The point-rating letter system for evaluating academic achievements is a system for
evaluating the level of academic achievements in points corresponding to the letter system with
a digital equivalent adopted in international practice, and allowing to establish the rating of
students;

3) Final certification of students is a procedure for assessing the theoretical level of

4) Credit learning technology is the learning based on student’s choice and independent

5) Current control of students' academic achievement is a systematic check of students'

according to the schedule during the academic period;
6) Intermediate certification of students is a procedure conducted during the examination
session in order to assess the quality of mastering (knowledge, abilities, skills and
competencies) by students of the entire volume or the part of the academic discipline content

final certifications of students and calculates their academic ratings;
10) Midterm control is a control of academic achievements of students at the end of a
section (module) of one academic discipline;
11) Grade Point Average (GPA) is the average assessment of the student’s level of
academic achievements for one academic year according to the chosen program (the ratio of
overall credits to the digital equivalent of intermediate certification evaluation of the disciplines
to the total number of credits for the current period of study);
12) Examination session is the period of intermediate certification of students in higher
educational institutions.

Paragraph 1. The procedure for conducting current control of students' academic
performance at the university

during online learning is conducted in accordance with the Standard Rules of activity of
educational organizations of corresponding types approved by the order no. 595 of the Minister
of Education and Science of the Republic of Kazakhstan dated October 30, 2018.
The current control of students' academic performance is conducted on each topic of the
academic discipline and includes knowledge control in classroom and extracurricular classes in
online and offline mode. The evaluation of current control of academic performance consists of
the grades obtained in online and offline classes.
12. The current control evaluations are submitted to the LMS Platonus electronic journal on
a weekly basis.
13. Assignments for current control included in syllabuses are sent and received via LMS
Canvas or corporate mail.

Paragraph 2. The procedure for conducting intermediate control of students' academic
performance at the university
21. Intermediate certification of students at the university is carried out in accordance with
the academic calendar, working curriculum and curricula developed on the basis of the SES and
standard curricula for educational programs of higher and postgraduate education.
22. Intermediate certification of students is conducted in the form of exams, defense and
reports on professional practice with obligatory evaluation and is defined as an examination
session.
23. In accordance with its Academic Policy, the university determines the procedure for
organizing and conducting intermediate certification of students.
24. Academic Council of the university approves the form of the examination for each
discipline no later than one month before the start of the session.
25. The Registrar's office organizes and conducts the intermediate certification (examination
session) of students.
26. The University conducts intermediate certification of students through LMS Canvas,
ASU "Turan" and other applied DET in order to evaluate the achievement of learning outcomes
within a specific discipline.
27. Intermediate certification of students is carried out online in the form of creative exams,
exams with an "open book" (take-home open book exam), oral exams, testing, protection of
reports on professional practice with mandatory assessment and is defined as an examination
session.
28. The Registrar's office organizes and conducts intermediate certification (examination
session) of students in the remote learning mode.

35. The Registrar's office prepares an academic rating of students according to the results of

results of the intermediate certification of an academic discipline are summed up. (Final
knowledge score % = 0.6*(P1% + P2%)/2+0,4* E%). The exam is evaluated on a 100-point
system. Students with an average score of at least 50 points according to the results of boundary
controls are allowed to intermediate certification.

the mastered credits in the established amount to the relevant academic discipline and is recorded
in the student's transcript.
38. In case of receiving an “unsatisfactory” corresponding to the “FX”, the student has the
opportunity to retake the final control without re-taking the program of the discipline/module.

54. A student who has not scored the minimum values of the transfer score and the credits

55. A student that has completed the course program in full, but has not scored the minimum
transfer point, in order to increase his average academic performance (GPA), is given the
opportunity to re-study certain disciplines on a paid basis in the summer semester and re-take
exams.
56. The final score is calculated again if the result of exam retake is positive, and recorded
in the examination sheet and transcript.
57. The latest grades in the academic discipline are taken into account while calculating the
average academic performance score.
58. All the final grades of the student, including the positive results of exam retakes, are
recorded in the transcript.

Paragraph 3. The procedure for conducting the “History of Kazakhstan” state exam
at the University
63. According to the academic calendar, all bachelor's degree program students take the
“History of Kazakhstan” state exam upon its completion, in the same academic period.
64. The department conducting classes on the academic discipline “History of Kazakhstan”
(hereinafter referred to as the department) together with the Dean's office of the faculty and the
registrar's office organizes the state exam. The department, based on a standard curriculum for
this discipline, approved by the Academic Council of the University, develops one working
curriculum for all forms of education and specialties, for conducting a state exam in the
discipline “History of Kazakhstan”
65. The form of the “History of Kazakhstan” state exam is determined by the Academic
Council of the University
66. To conduct the “History of Kazakhstan” state exam, on the faculty dean’s
recommendation, the state examination commission for one calendar year (hereinafter referred
to as the SEC) is formed
67. The Chairman and the composition of the SEC on the discipline “History of
Kazakhstan” are approved by the order of the rector on the basis of the Academic Council’s
decision.
68. The schedule of meetings of the SEC is compiled by the registrar's office in accordance
with the academic calendar and approved by the rector no later than two weeks before the start
of the state exam.
69. The duration of the SEC meeting does not exceed 6 (six) academic hours per day.
70. The university determines the order of organization and conducting the state exam on
the “History of Kazakhstan” academic discipline, as well as the procedure for the SEC meeting.
71. The state exam results are evaluated according to the point - rating letter system of
evaluation of students' academic achievements with their transfer to the traditional scale of
students' knowledge evaluation. The minutes of the SEC meeting are compiled in accordance
with Appendix 3. The SE grade is indicated in the minutes in percentage and in the letter system
with their transfer into the traditional evaluation system.
72. At the same time, the final evaluation is made taking into account the assessments of the
admission rating and the state exam.
73. If the student receives “unsatisfactory” at the state exam on the discipline “History of
Kazakhstan”, he/she is re-enrolled in this discipline on a paid basis in the next academic period
or summer semester, re-attends all types of academic sessions, fulfills the requirements of the
current control, receives admission and retakes the state exam.
74. Retaking a positive assessment of the state exam in the discipline "History of
Kazakhstan" in order to improve it is not allowed.
75. A student that does not agree with the state exam result submits an appeal no later than
the next day after the SE.

university, where he indicates the selected form of final certification (in case of choosing the 1st
form, he also indicates the topic of the thesis / project). The number of students defending a
thesis or a graduation project is no more than 50% of the total number of graduate students.
