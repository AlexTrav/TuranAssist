### Questions and Answers
Everything that might be useful before admission and during your studies
How do I register for the Turan Electronic Library?
To register, you need to log in to the electronic library using your university corporate email. Authorization automatically confirms your registration in the system.
What electronic resources are available to students and faculty?
Users have access to the Turan Electronic Library, REME (Russian: РМЭБ), Web of Science, Wiley Online Library, IPR SMART, Urait (Юрайт), the "Paragraph" legal databases, and other scientific and educational resources. Some of these resources can be accessed remotely after registering with your corporate email.
Can I take books home?
Yes, books can be checked out for home use through the circulation desk (abonement). However, the final or sole copy of any publication cannot be taken home. Materials from the reading room must be used within the library only.
How do I find a specific book or textbook?
You can search using the Turan Library electronic catalog, which includes textbooks, study guides, monographs, dissertations, and electronic publications by the university faculty.
Can alumni use the library after graduation?
Yes, alumni are welcome to visit the reading room, use the library catalog, and access open electronic resources. They also have access to a number of subscription-based electronic databases while on university campus. However, access to the corporate email is deactivated once the student status changes to alumnus.
