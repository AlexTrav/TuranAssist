REGULATIONS ON THE PROCEDURE FOR AWARDING AVAILABLE EDUCATIONAL GRANTS RELEASED IN THE PROCESS OF OBTAINING HIGHER EDUCATION
⠀
The regulation of awarding educational grants available under programs of higher education
⠀
The Regulation defines the procedure for awarding available educational grants released in the process of obtaining higher education at Turan University.⠀
⠀
1.1. GENERAL PROVISIONS
- These Rules for awarding an educational grant to pay for higher education (hereinafter referred to as the Rules) have been developed in accordance with subparagraph 5) of Article 4 of the Law of the Republic of Kazakhstan dated July 27, 2007 “On Education” and determine the procedure for awarding educational grants to pay for higher education.
- The following basic concepts are used in these Rules:
⠀
1) educational grant – a target amount of money provided to a student on the terms established by the legislation of the republic of Kazakhstan to pay for vocational education;
2) authorized body in the field of education – the central executive body of the Republic of Kazakhstan, which carries out management and intersectoral coordination in the field of education;
3) republican competition commission – a commission established by the authorized body in the field of education to conduct a competition for the award of educational grants (hereinafter referred to as the Commission);⠀
⠀
THE PROCEDURE FOR AWARDING AVAILABLE EDUCATIONAL GRANTS RELEASED IN THE PROCESS OF OBTAINING HIGHER EDUCATION
⠀
2.1. Available educational grants released in the process of obtaining higher education, in the context of training specialties, are awarded on a competitive basis to students on a fee-based basis in this specialty. The competition is held based on the results of an interim certification with the issuance of a certificate of the award of an educational grant.
In the case of identical indicators of the average academic performance during the competition for available educational grants, students who have grades only A, A- (“excellent”), then grades from A, A- (“excellent”) to B+, B, B- (“good”) for the entire period of study have a preferential right.
⠀
2.2. The award of available educational grants, released in the process of obtaining higher education, is carried out during the summer and winter holidays, on the available available places on a competitive basis, in the following order:
1) a student on a paid basis submits an application addressed to the head of the university for further training under an educational grant;
2) the university, having considered this application on a competitive basis, together with the decision of the academic council, sends it to the authorized body in the field of education for decision-making by August 5 and January 15 of the current year. The student’s application, together with the decision of the academic council, is accompanied by an extract from the student’s record book or transcript, a copy of his identity document, and a certificate of the holder of an educational grant (original) expelled from the university;
⠀
2.3. in the absence of applicants for a specific specialty, available educational grants released in the process of obtaining higher education are transferred on a competitive basis within the training area based on the results of the summer and winter examination sessions;
3) the authorized body in the field of education reviews the received documents in the context of specialties, forms and terms of study, taking into account the year of admission and, if the issue is resolved positively, issues an order on awarding an educational grant;
4) on the basis of the order of the authorized body in the field of education, a certificate of the award of an educational grant is issued;
5) on the basis of the issued certificate of the award of an educational grant, the head of the university issues an order for further education under the educational grant.⠀

APPLICATION FOR STUDENT RESIDENCE
Places in the SR are allocated to those in need of housing (socially unprotected persons, persons from other regions of the republic of Kazakhstan and foreign citizens) for living during a certain academic period, excluding vacations.
When allocating places , the applications of the following categories of students are considered first of all:
- orphans;
- persons with disabilities, including people with disabilities of I and II categories;
- 1st and 2nd year students enrolled in the state educational grant;
- students from large families;
- 1st and 2nd year students who come from remote regions and districts (not applicable to holders of internal University grants for the full term of study);
- exchange students in the framework of academic mobility;
- students with good academic performance who take an active part in the social life of the University.
