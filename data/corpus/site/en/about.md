### Foundation
Turan University was established and registered in 1992 in Almaty and is the core of the educational corporation. An important advantage of Turan University is the full cycle of education: lyceum-college-undergraduate (29 educational programs) – Master's (27 educational programs) – Doctoral (6 educational programs) and MBA and DBA programs.
The university has dissertation councils for the defense of PhD dissertations.
Turan University is an innovative-entrepreneurial university. In this context, the university carries out extensive work to involve staff, faculty, and students themselves in innovative activities in the fields of education, science, and society.
Students can study under state grants and grants from the university rector. High-achieving students can apply for scholarships from Kazakhstani and foreign companies and organizations.
### Digital Space
The university has more than 1 500 personal computers. The internal system is supported by a powerful server. Turan University became the first higher education institution in the Republic of Kazakhstan to join the Eduroam project, which allows students and university staff to use free Wi-Fi in almost any university across 54 countries worldwide using their corporate login.
The university also opened the "Apple Digital Lab" digital laboratory. As part of the project, children and youth have access to educational programs developed by Apple – "Everyone Can Code" and "Everyone Can Create.
