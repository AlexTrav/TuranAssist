### Reception of foreign citizens
Reception of foreign citizens
- Information about Turan University (https://drive.google.com/file/d/1GhaN_fgQC7TEI9sv13zwQl7ESo3inF-G/view)
For foreign citizens, admission takes place on the basis of an interview with the admissions committee.
If a citizen of another country has graduated from school in Kazakhstan, then admission takes place according to the UNT.
The documents are accepted from June 1 to August 25.
LIST OF DOCUMENTS:
- Application (application form), filled in Russian with an attached colored photo;
- Notarized copies of the document on education and its supplement (with a translation of the document on education and its supplement into the state or Russian language). The translation is certified by a notary on the territory of the Republic of Kazakhstan;
- An identity document, a notarized copy of the document (with translation into the state or Russian languages). The translation is certified by a notary on the territory of the Republic of Kazakhstan;
- A copy of the license and / or certificate of accreditation of the educational institution that issued the document on education;
- Certificate of recognition and nostrification of educational documents
- Health certificate No. 075 (HIV, malaria, fecal tests) All medical certificates are made on the territory of the Republic of Kazakhstan;
- Apostille of a college diploma (for holders of secondary special education)
- Photo 3*4 – 6 pieces
Educational documents issued outside the territory of the Republic of Kazakhstan undergo the nostrification procedure
When submitting an application for recognition and nostrification of educational documents, the following documents are attached to the PSC:
- Application for recognition of educational documents;
- Notarized copies of the document on education and its supplement (with the translation of the document on education and its supplement into the state or Russian language), the translation is certified by a notary on the territory of the Republic of Kazakhstan;
- An identity document, a notarized copy of the document (with translation into the state or Russian languages), the translation is certified by a notary on the territory of the Republic of Kazakhstan;
- A copy of the license and / or certificate of accreditation of the educational institution that issued the document on education;
- A document certifying the identity of the authorized representative (for identification) and a document certifying the authority to represent- if representative of the recipient’s services applies;
- Payment receipt.
