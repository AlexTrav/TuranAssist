### Inclusive education
- At the request of students, special educational needs are taken into account when drawing up the class schedule (on the first floor, etc.);
- There is access to textbooks for study both in the library and at home, and teaching materials are also provided in electronic form;
- Provided the opportunity to develop optimal individual educational trajectories for students with special educational needs;
- If necessary, an individual training schedule is drawn up;
- Access to the IPR BOOKS electronic library system is available;
- Volunteer assistance is organized;
- Discounts on tuition fees are provided for persons with disabilities (Ⅰ - Ⅲ group).

- Tactile tiles

- Tactile mnemonic diagram
- Medical and health center
- Information boards for people with hearing impairments
- Designated parking lot
- Elevator adapted for people with disabilities

### Support Center for Students with Special Educational Needs (SEN)
- The support center for students with special educational needs is located in the university library;
- The reading room is equipped with workstations for users with SEN;
- The university has an annual subscription to the IPR SMART educational resource. The subscription provides access to mobile applications for the iOS and Android operating systems;
- Each computer at the workstation for users with SEN is equipped with applications:
- IPRbooks Mobile Reader is a mobile application that provides the most efficient and convenient work for users with the electronic library system on a smartphone or tablet.
- IPRbooks WV-Reader is a specialized application for use by people with visual disabilities. The application has an adapted catalog of educational literature for people with visual disabilities, full voice guidance for the application, and the ability to connect a Braille keyboard.

### SPECIAL TOOLS FOR USE can be obtained in the library reading room (immediately or upon prior request)
- LEVENHUK DTX 43 Digital Magnifier (Levenhuk)
Levenhuk Digital Magnifier is an optical instrument with the ability to magnify images up to 14x and supports 7 color modes for text display for comfortable reading. The magnifier allows you to connect to a computer, take screenshots while working and save them to a memory card. It can be powered either from the mains or from a built-in battery, which provides up to 120 minutes of battery life. Place the digital magnifier over the text and move it as you read. The magnified image is instantly displayed on the built-in screen. While working, you can easily change the magnification and select the appropriate color mode. Additionally, the device is equipped with a screenshot function - with one press of a button, you will save the image to the magnifier's memory. Later, you can copy it to your computer or save it to your hard drive. The digital magnifier is also suitable for lectures and presentations: it transmits the image to an external screen in real time, making it a universal tool for studying and working.
- Magnifying ruler
The magnifying ruler is a tool that combines the functions of a ruler and a magnifying glass. To use the magnifying ruler, simply place it over the text or document you want to read. Slowly move the ruler along the lines to enlarge the text. You can also use the ruler scale to measure objects.
- Hand magnifier
The 50 mm diameter magnifier has a three-fold magnification. The classic hand magnifier is a universal tool for working with small text or images.
- Portable information induction system "Tiflostar TFC-2"
Portable information induction system is a device designed to improve the perception of sound information. It is especially useful for users with hearing impairments. The system allows you to clearly hear speech and sounds in the room, eliminating background noise. To use the system, turn on the hearing aid and switch it to the "T" mode (telecoil). Then go into the induction loop zone, and the sound will be transmitted clearly and without interference.
- Keyboard with tactile markings (Russian layout)
Keyboard with tactile markings is a device that allows users to enter text using relief keys, each of which corresponds to a Braille symbol. The keyboard is connected to the computer using a USB cable, suitable for working with texts, educational programs, and for performing other tasks. Each key on the device has convex marks, which allows the user to recognize symbols by touch.
- Keyboard with tactile markings (Kazakh language layout)
A keyboard with tactile markings is a device that allows users to enter text using raised keys, each of which corresponds to a Braille symbol. The keyboard is connected to a computer using a USB cable and is suitable for working with texts, educational programs, and other tasks. Each key on the device has raised marks, which allows the user to recognize symbols by touch.
- Special headphones for people with hearing impairments
- Audio speakers, voice recorders
