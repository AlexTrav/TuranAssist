OPEN ACCESS RESOURCES
APA PsycNet (https://psycnet.apa.org/home) - the premier international database from the American Psychological Association. It features over 6 million records, including peer-reviewed journals, books, and reports. The platform offers extensive Open Access options for research in psychology, behavioral, and social sciences.
ACM Digital Library (https://dl.acm.org/) is a premier research platform for computing and information technology. The database brings together nearly 4 million scholarly publications, including journals, conference proceedings, and newsletters from the Association for Computing Machinery dating back to 1908. Under the Open Access model (Digital Library Basic), over 800,000 full-text works are available for free reading and download to users worldwide.

Taylor & Francis (https://www.tandfonline.com/) – An international publishing company specializing in academic publications. Over 200,000 articles are available in Open Access.
Springer (https://link.springer.com/) – A publisher of scientific content. Over 460,000 documents are available in Open Access.
eLIBRARY.RU (http://eLIBRARY.RU) – An electronic library of scientific publications with extensive search and analysis capabilities for scientific information. 4,500 scientific journals are available in free open access. Registration on the website allows you to view full-text documents and create your own collections.

UNESCO Digital Library (https://unesdoc.unesco.org/home) – A collection of publications, documents, and other materials about UNESCO’s activities (in education, natural, social and human sciences, culture, communication, and information). It contains more than 350,000 documents dating back to 1945.

### Alumni
Resources for “Turan” Corporation Alumni
Reading Room: Alumni are welcome to visit the library’s reading room during its regular working hours. They may use the available computers or work on their own laptops, as well as participate in book exhibitions and library events.
Turan Electronic Library: The electronic library is available through the ASU “Turan” system and requires a corporate account for access. Since corporate email access is terminated after graduation, alumni may contact library staff for assistance in searching for the literature they need in the electronic catalog. Library staff will help locate the required materials and provide information about their availability.
Literature: Books and periodicals (newspapers and magazines) are available for use in the reading room only. An ID card or passport is required.
Subscribed Electronic Resources: Alumni can access the university’s subscribed electronic resources on campus through the university network using IP-based access.
