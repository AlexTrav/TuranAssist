Worldwide master’s degrees are post-graduate education for students who already have a bachelor’s degree.
The specialized master’s degree (1 year) provides advanced professional training. The training is of a practical nature and is aimed at developing management skills in all areas of activity. The task of the specialized magistracy is to prepare professional managers and leaders.
The Scientific and Pedagogical Magistracy (2 years) provides in-depth scientific and pedagogical training. Graduates of The Scientific and Pedagogical Magistracy can also teach at universities.
### Directions and length of master‘s degree
The Scientific and Pedagogical (previous level of education – bachelor ‘s degree or higher special education without work experience), study period – 2 years.
Professionally oriented degree (previous level of education – bachelor ‘s degree or higher special education), with a study period – 1 year.
Form of education: full- time
Languages of training: Kazakh, Russian.
Academic degree:
- Research and pedagogical Master’s degree: Master of Science MSc (in the corresponding specialty).
- Specialized Master’s degree (on the corresponding specialty).
For research and pedagogical Master’s degree programs – at least 75 points out of 150, with a minimum of 7 points in each subject;
For specialized Master’s degree programs – at least 30 points out of 75, with a minimum of 7 points in each subject.
### Admission of documents to the master school
In accordance with the amendments, admission to master’s and doctoral studies will now take place twice a year: traditionally in summer and additionally in winter.
Acceptance of documents in winter:
Registration for participation in the second comprehensive Master’s degree testing takes place via the website of the National Testing Center – app.testcenter.kz .
Comprehensive testing: from November 19 to December 11. The passing score in the scientific and pedagogical master’s degree is 75, in the profile – 30.
In case of successful completion of the tests, applicants will be able to win grants that were not considered following the results of the summer admission. Documents for participation in the competition for the award of educational grants will be accepted until December 25.
Admission to the Master’s program: from December 26 to January 10.
### 1st stage
From June 1 to July 8 of this year, submit the required package of documents to the admissions office of the university.
- Application addressed to the rector
- A copy of the document on higher education (diplomas with attachments)
- Personal sheet of personnel records and a document confirming labor activity (for persons with work experience)
- A copy of the identity document
- Photos 3 × 4 (6 pieces).
- Medical certificate form 075-U, approved by order of the Acting Minister of Health of the Republic of Kazakhstan dated November 23, 2010 No. 907 “On approval of forms of primary medical documentation of healthcare organizations” (registered in the Register of State Registration of Normative Legal Acts under No. 6697) (hereinafter – Order No. 907);
- A copy of the international certificate confirming proficiency in a foreign language in accordance with the common European competences (standards) of proficiency in a foreign language (if any).
Together with copies of documents, their originals are submitted for verification. After verification, the originals are returned.
Persons who have received education documents in foreign educational organizations, for admission to the magistracy, must go through the procedure of recognition or nostrification of education documents. Higher education documents submitted in a foreign language must have a notarized translation into Kazakh / Russian.

### Educational prescription programs
If there is a discrepancy between the profile of the educational program of the magistracy and the applicant’s educational program of higher education, prerequisites are set for the master’s student upon admission to the magistracy. Applicants are allowed to master them on their own and take exams on a paid basis after passing the entrance exams. Prerequisite programs for educational master’s programs are developed with the relevant departments.
