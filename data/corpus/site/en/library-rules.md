### Terms of the library use
Access to the Electronic Library is provided through ASU “Turan” from any device with an internet connection. Access to the EL is granted automatically via the user’s corporate email when logging into ASU “Turan”.
The literature is issued on the basis of an identity card.
In the reading room, readers use all types of publications without the right to take out.
The delivery of literature to the house is made on a subscription. The last or only copy is not subject to delivery to the house.
Not allowed:
- Visit the library in outerwear, bring food, break the silence and order
- Transfer the right to use the identity card to other persons for obtaining literature
- Take out literature from the library, without registering with the librarian
- Damage library publications and property
- To enter the office premises, the book depository without the permission of the librarian.
