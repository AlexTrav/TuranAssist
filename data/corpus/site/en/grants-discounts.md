### Grants and discounts
All discounts and grants are provided in accordance with the "Rules for Awarding Internal Educational Grants and Applying the Discount System at Turan University."
Internal grants for study in the English-medium programs (covering 1, 2, or 4 years of tuition) are awarded based on the tuition fee of the corresponding Kazakh- or Russian-medium educational program.
If the tuition fee for the English-medium program exceeds that of the corresponding Kazakh- or Russian-medium program, the student is required to pay the difference annually at their own expense.
Internal grants and discounts of Turan University do not apply to dual degree programs.
If an applicant is eligible for more than one type of discount, only the highest discount will be granted. Discounts are not cumulative.
### Government grants
Hundreds of students (lucky holders of state grants) who have chosen us among other universities enter our university every year.
Government grants are allocated annually for educational programs throughout Kazakhstan. When applying for participation in the state grant competition, you need to specify the code of the university where you plan to study (the code of our university is 093). As a rule, state grants are distributed among the highest UNT scores. And if you become the grant holder, it goes to the university that you specified when applying.
In order to participate in the state grant competition, you need to dial:
- at least 75 points in the field of education “Law” (at least 5 points in the History of Kazakhstan and specialized subjects and at least 3 points in Reading Literacy and Mathematical Literacy)
- at least 50 points in other educational programs (at least 5 points in History of Kazakhstan and specialized subjects and at least 3 points in Reading Literacy and Mathematical Literacy)

### "TURAN ÚMITI" Grant
The "TURAN ÚMITI" Internal Educational Grant is awarded annually based on the results of a competitive selection process.
The following grants are awarded to the winners:
- 1st place – 4 years of free tuition;
- 2nd place – 2 years of free tuition;
- 3rd place – 1 year of free tuition.
The competition is open to applicants who have scored 80 or more points on the Unified National Testing (UNT) (out of 140), as well as college graduates applying to a related educational program who have scored 40 or more points on the UNT (out of 70) or 40 or more points on the university's internal entrance examination.
Selection Criteria
The following criteria are considered during the competitive selection process:
- Holder of the "Altyn Belgi" award;
- UNT certificate score or internal entrance examination score;
- Grade Point Average (GPA) / average grade of the secondary school certificate or college diploma;
- Participation in international and national subject Olympiads and scientific competitions approved by the Ministry of Education and Science of the Republic of Kazakhstan, as well as subject competitions and Olympiads organized by Turan University;
- Valid international language certificates (IELTS or TOEFL iBT) issued no more than two years before the date of application.
For the most up-to-date information on registration deadlines, please contact the Turan University Admissions Office. To participate in the competition, applicants must submit their documents to the University.
The Competition Committee reserves the right to change the amount of the grant awarded to individual applicants based on the evaluation of their portfolio.
### Grant Project
Turan University gives applicants the opportunity to receive grants for free education. According to the results of the competition the following grants are provided:
1 place – 4 years of free education
2nd place – 2 years of free education
3rd place – 1 year of free education.
The competition runs for several months and covers all of Kazakhstan to give everyone the opportunity to participate.
Stage 1 – Register on the website and write an essay on “How I see the future of my country and what I can do for it. Send the essay to grant@turan-edu.kz
Stage 2 – Test for logic and critical thinking. The test is held online. Password and login are sent in advance to the e-mail of a participant who passed the previous stage successfully.
Stage 3 – interview with the members of the competition committee. Participants who have successfully passed the previous stages of the competition are admitted to the interview. The interview is conducted in person for residents of Almaty and Almaty region and remotely for residents of other regions. The date and time of the interview will be determined individually for each participant, the details will be sent by e-mail.

### Master's Degree Discounts and Grants
The following admission benefits are available for applicants to the research and pedagogical master's degree programs:
- Graduates of Turan University holding an honors diploma are granted a 25% discount on the annual tuition fee. Graduates holding a standard diploma are granted a 10% discount on the annual tuition fee.
- Graduates of other universities who are admitted to the master's degree program with an honors diploma are granted a 15% discount on the annual tuition fee for the first year of study.
For students enrolled in research and pedagogical master's degree programs (2-year duration), academic performance discounts are awarded annually as follows:
- Students with a GPA of 4.0 and no grade lower than A− in their transcript receive a 20% discount based on the tuition fee of the previous academic year.
- Students with a GPA of 3.67 or higher receive a 10% discount based on the tuition fee of the previous academic year.
The "TURAN ÚMITI" Internal Educational Grant is awarded annually based on the results of a competitive selection process.
The competition is open to applicants who have scored 75 or more points on the Comprehensive Testing (CT). Applicants to master's degree programs in educational groups requiring creative preparation are eligible to participate based on the results of the Comprehensive Testing (CT) and the creative entrance examinations, provided they score 75 or more points.
The Competition Committee takes the following criteria into account when evaluating applicants:
- Comprehensive Testing (CT) score;
- Bachelor's degree GPA / average diploma grade;
- Foreign language proficiency confirmed by internationally recognized language certificates;
- Personal achievements in scientific research and sports.
The Competition Committee reserves the right to adjust the amount of the grant awarded to individual applicants based on the evaluation of their portfolio.
