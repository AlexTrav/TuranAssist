### Information about the education documents of Turan University’s sample format
The diplomas of the “Turan” University and their supplements are printed by the banknote factory of the National Bank of the Republic of Kazakhstan.
The forms and appendices to the education documents of Turan University’s sample format contain:
- graduate’s surname, first name, patronymic (if any);
- the code and name of the educational program (specialty);
- the degree awarded (if any);
- the date and number of the certification commission meeting’s minutes on awarding the degree (if any).
The University diploma is protected from forgery by protective watermarks, a unique identification number generated by the Ministry of Education and Science of the Republic of Kazakhstan and registered in the National Educational Database, and a QR code with the ability to verify the authenticity of the diploma on the website of the Ministry of Education and Science of the Republic of Kazakhstan.
The logos of the university and IAAR accreditation agency for institutional accreditation are placed in the education documents of “Turan” University's own sample.
The logos of the university and IAAR accreditation agency for specialized accreditation are placed in the appendix to the documents of “Turan” University's own sample.
The forms and appendices to the education documents of “Turan” University's own sample are filled out in the State, English and Russian languages.
The document on the formation of “Turan” university’s own sample is signed by the rector, the chairman of the certification commission and the technical secretary.
The appendices to the documents on education of “Turan” University's own sample contain the signatures of the rector, dean and technical secretary.
