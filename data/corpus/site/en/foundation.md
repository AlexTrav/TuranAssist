Foundation – a reliable foundation for successful university admission and studies!
The Foundation Pre-University Training Center is not only an important stage of preparation for higher education, but also a smart investment in your future. The Center's program is designed to equip students with all the necessary academic and language skills, including specialized courses for foreign nationals in Kazakh and Russian, facilitating rapid adaptation to life in Kazakhstan and integration into the country's academic and cultural environment. In addition, the Foundation Pre-University Training Center offers preparation courses for the Comprehensive Testing (CT) exam for master's degree applicants, as well as KAZTEST preparation courses for doctoral applicants and foreign nationals planning to obtain a residence permit in the Republic of Kazakhstan.
### Why choose Turan Foundation?
- Individual approach and support at every stage of learning.
- Experienced instructors who will help you achieve your goals.
- Extensive opportunities to develop language and academic skills.
- Preparation for the Comprehensive Testing (CT) exam for master's degree applicants.
- Preparation for the KAZTEST exam for doctoral applicants and residence permit seekers.
